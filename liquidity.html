How this is checked

The pools

A Solada coin that reaches its graduation target opens its own Minswap pool, with ADA on one side and the coin on the other. Gator Eyes reads every pool from the chain every 10 minutes: the ADA and tokens inside it and the LP tokens that stand for it. Coins still on their bonding curve have no pool yet. Snek.fun pools are not listed here.

Who holds the LP

Whoever holds a pool's LP tokens can trade them back for its ADA and tokens. Solada means a wallet Solada publishes on its proof-of-reserves page. Creator is the coin's launcher wallet. Burned is LP sent to the Cardano Graveyard, which can never be taken out. Anything else is another wallet. Solada's terms say it owns each coin's pool LP and only withdraws the pool's fee growth, never the principal. Removals by its wallets show below so you can check.

Changes

Added and Removed mean a wallet's LP and the pool's total LP grew or shrank together. Moved means LP changed hands without leaving the pool, and Burned means it went to the Graveyard. Ordinary buys and sells change a pool's balance but are not liquidity changes. Changes are found by comparing each read with the last, so the list starts when this tracker went live and times are accurate to about 10 minutes.

Depth and price impact

Depth is the ADA in the pool as a share of the coin's market cap right now. The 1,000 ADA column is how far a single 1,000 ADA buy would move the price in that pool, including its trading fee. Bigger pools move less. It ignores trades routed through other venues.

Pool balances, LP holders and changes come from Cardano through Koios. Coin names, market caps and settings come from Solada.fun. Address and transaction links open Cardanoscan; coin links open Solada with our referral code.
