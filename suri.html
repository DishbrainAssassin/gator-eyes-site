What we can't do for free

Gator Eyes runs lean: it reads public trades every two minutes and adds them up. That's how it stays free. The jobs below need heavier systems, constant upkeep or handling people's trades, so they belong in a paid tool. Rather than put them behind a paywall, we point you to Suri.

  • Safety checks on every coin

    Scoring each new coin in seconds and keeping those scores right takes nonstop scanning and judgment calls. Gator Eyes reports trades, it doesn't rate coins.

    Check a coin on Suri

  • Wallet profiles and PnL

    Profit, win rate and smart-money lists mean building a history for every wallet. We don't follow or rank wallets.

    Look up wallets on Suri

  • Copy trading and swaps

    Anything that places trades needs wallet connections and the responsibility that comes with them. Gator Eyes never asks for your wallet.

    Trade tools on Suri

  • Other launchpads

    Watching dgens.fun and snek.fun means running more trackers. Gator Eyes stays the Solada one.

    Multi-launchpad on Suri
