Nov 13, 2026

Fast Lunge now uses the Run Downs rule

Fast Lunge alerts now use the same target as Gator Run Downs:

  • 15+ different buyers in the coin's first hour
  • 1,000+ net ADA in (buys minus sells)
  • The creator's buys don't count toward either

The alert fires the moment both are hit. Each coin gets one alert, and the rule is the same on Solada and Snek.fun.

What changed: the old alert needed 1,000 ADA of buys from 8+ wallets, and sells didn't count against it. The new bar is higher, so expect fewer Fast Lunges. When one fires, the coin has hit the game's targets.

Playing Run Downs? An alert means both targets are met at that moment. The race still settles at the end of the Sprint, so a coin can still finish No Lunge if sells pull net ADA back under 1,000.

Nov 12, 2026

The Gator Eyes extension is live

Gator Eyes for Cardano is now on the Chrome Web Store, free. On any Solada or Snek.fun coin page, it adds a small panel with:

  • 24h volume and net flow, with the USDCx value
  • Solana pool 24h volume for Solada coins that also trade on Solana
  • Gator Eyes alerts for that coin, plus what the price did 1h and 24h later
  • Creator watch, the next unlock, and liquidity for graduated coins
  • Ask Quagmire, right in the panel
  • Customize: pick which sections show

There's also a trade ribbon along the bottom of the page (live trades, open Run Downs races or your ☆ watchlist), and a Run Downs tab in the toolbar where you can make free Lunge / No Lunge picks. No token gate.

It never reads wallets, cookies or what you type on the page. Already installed it? Chrome updates it for you.

Nov 12, 2026

Names on the holders list

The holders page now shows ADA Handles. If one of a coin's 15 biggest wallets has an ADA Handle, its $name appears above the address and links to handle.me. This works for Solada and Snek.fun coins, and the CSV download includes the handles too.

A handle is a name the wallet's owner registered for themselves. It doesn't confirm who they are. Pools, bonding curves, locks and Solada's own wallets are skipped.

Nov 1, 2026

Gator Eyes on your phone

Add Gator Eyes to your home screen and choose which alerts buzz your phone: whale trades, volume spikes, creator sells, fast launches and $GAEY trades.

Choose Solada.fun, Snek.fun or both. You can filter to your watchlist too; brand-new coins still come through. Need a break? Mute alerts for a while on the app page and they resume automatically.

On iPhone: open the app page in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the new icon and tap Turn on alerts. Requires iOS 16.4 or newer.

No sign-up, no wallet connection, no App Store download. Gator Eyes alerts never ask you to connect a wallet, sign anything or send ADA.
