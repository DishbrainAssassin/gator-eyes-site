What the gator watches

  1. Whale trades

    Any trade worth $500 or more in ADA, or a trade worth at least 5% of a coin's market cap.

  2. Volume spikes

    A coin trading four times its normal pace over 15 minutes, with at least 1,500 ADA moving.

  3. Creator sells

    The wallet that created a coin selling 100 ADA or more. Posted as a record, not a warning.

  4. Fast launches

    New coins that pull in 1,000 ADA from eight or more wallets in their first hour.

  5. Gator Tracks

    A daily recap at 9 AM Eastern: volume, most traded coins, and where the money went.
