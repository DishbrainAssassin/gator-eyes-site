What do these alerts mean?

What the gator watches

  1. Whale trades

    Any trade worth $1,000 or more in ADA, or a trade worth at least 7.5% of a coin's market cap (minimum 250 ADA).

  2. Volume spikes

    A coin trading four times its normal pace over 15 minutes, with at least 1,500 ADA moving.

  3. Creator sells

    The wallet that created a coin selling 500 ADA or more, or at least 5% of its market cap. Posted as a record, not a warning.

  4. Hatchlings

    Every new coin, posted once soon after it goes live. A record that it exists, not a pick.

  5. Fast Lunges

    Fast launches: new coins that pull in 1,000 ADA from eight or more wallets in their first hour.

  6. Gator Tracks

    A daily recap at 12:01 AM Eastern, right after the day closes: volume, most traded coins, and where the money went, split by launchpad with a combined total.
