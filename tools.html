Watching Solada and Snek.fun

Tools

Free tools for Solada.fun and Snek.fun projects and the people who trade them. No sign-up and no wallet connection.

For everyone

Phone alerts

Put Gator Eyes on your home screen and get whale trades, volume spikes, creator sells and fast launches as phone notifications. Pick which ones, or only coins on your watchlist. Works on iPhone and Android.

 For projects

Holder snapshot

See where a coin's supply sits: the curve, the pool, locks, the creator, contracts and the wallets that actually hold it. Then turn those wallets into an airdrop list with a minimum balance or "held since" rule, and download the CSV.

 For research

Trade history

Every buy and sell for one coin, newest first: time, side, ADA, tokens, price paid and the wallet. Filter by side, size or a single wallet, load the full history and download the CSV.

 For everyone

Unlock calendar

Every Solada.fun token lock, verified on Cardano itself and laid out by the day it opens. Each one shows its share of supply and ADA value, with creator locks marked, so a big unlock day is never a surprise.

 For projects

Stat card

A ready-to-post image of any coin's last 24 hours: volume, net flow, price change, graduation progress and alerts.

 For everyone

Watchlist

Star any Solada.fun or Snek.fun coin and see price, flows, graduation progress and every alert on it in one view. Saved in your browser, with a link to carry it to another device.

 For everyone

Daily flow card

One image of a whole day on Solada.fun, sized for X and Discord.

 For websites

Alerts widget

Put live Gator Eyes alerts for your coin on your own site.
