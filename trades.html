How to read this

Where the trades come from

Solada coins: the trades Solada reports, on its bonding curve before graduation and on the Minswap pool after. Snek.fun coins: trades on its Splash curve and pool. Wallet-to-wallet transfers and trades on other DEXs are not included. Solada Pump coins also trade on Solana (Pump.fun or PumpSwap): Solada only publishes their ADA-side trades, so for those coins the page adds the Solana pool's last 24 hours of trades from GeckoTerminal, marked Solana, with amounts converted to ADA (~) at today's rate.

Price

ADA paid per token in that single trade (ADA divided by tokens), so it includes slippage and fees. It may not match the chart price at that moment.

How far back

The newest trades load first. Load older trades a batch at a time, or load everything, up to the most recent 5,000 trades. Times are Eastern.

Trades are read live from the coin's launchpad when you load the page. Wallet links open Cardanoscan. Gator Eyes doesn't rate coins or wallets. Information, not financial advice.
