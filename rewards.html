How this is worked out

Where the numbers come from

Solada publishes each coin's lifetime rewards paid to its holders and its launcher, with no history. Gator Eyes saves those totals every midnight Eastern, so yesterday is the difference between two saved days and this week is the difference over the last saved week. History starts Nov 12, 2026. Today so far compares Solada's live total with last midnight's.

Paid in

Each launcher picks which token their holder rewards are paid in: ADA, $SOLADA, $NIGHT, $STRIKE, SURF and others. SURF still uses its old on-chain ticker (FLOW), so it's shown as SURF. Amounts are the ADA value Solada records. When a coin pays in two tokens, half its ADA is counted toward each, because Solada doesn't publish the split.

Per 1k mcap

ADA paid to holders this week per 1,000 ADA of the coin's market cap right now, scaled to 7 days. It shows how much a coin's trading is sending to holders for its size. It is not a yield promise: it moves with volume and price every day.

Shared tickers

When more than one coin on Solada or Snek.fun uses the same ticker, a Ticker shared tag opens a list of every copy with its stage, holders, age, policy ID and whether the copies came from the same creator wallet. Gator Eyes doesn't say which one is real. Check the policy ID against the project's own links.

Reward totals, holder shares, reward tokens and market caps come from Solada.fun. Wallet balances come from Cardano through Koios; $handles are looked up on handle.me. Snek.fun coins appear in bag lookups and the shared-ticker list, but Snek.fun doesn't publish holder-reward totals. Coin links open Solada with our referral code. Gator Eyes doesn't rate coins.
