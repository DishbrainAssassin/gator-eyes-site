Privacy policy

Gator Eyes doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no wallet connection, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share anything about you. This page covers gatoreyes.fun and the Gator Eyes browser extension.

Browser extension

What it reads
Only the address of the tab when you're on solada.fun or snek.fun, so it knows which coin page you're looking at. It never reads page contents, what you type, cookies, passwords or your wallet.
What it sends
Nothing about you. It downloads the same public market data gatoreyes.fun uses (coin totals and recent alerts) and matches the coin on your own computer. The page address is never sent anywhere.
What it stores
Whether you collapsed the panel, plus a copy of the public data for about a minute so it doesn't download it again. Both stay in your browser.
Other sites
It doesn't run on any site except solada.fun and snek.fun, and it never changes or clicks anything on those pages.

gatoreyes.fun

Saved in your browser
Your watchlist, launchpad choice, beginner-mode setting and "since you last looked" snapshot are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you make a share link yourself.
No tracking
No analytics, no ad trackers and no third-party cookies.
Service providers
The site is hosted on Render, the data feed runs on Cloudflare, fonts load from Fontshare and Google Fonts, and some Solana coin prices come from DexScreener. Like any web host, they may log technical details such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect their services.
Addresses you type
Addresses you enter in a tool, like the Holders page's leave-out list, are used in your browser for that view only. Gator Eyes doesn't save them.
Ask Quagmire
The site helper answers from the public data feed inside your browser. Your questions aren't sent anywhere, and the chat clears when you close the tab.

Everything else

Children
Gator Eyes isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
Changes
If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
Contact
Questions? Reach us in the Gator Eyes Discord.
