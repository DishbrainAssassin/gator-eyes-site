Privacy policy

Gator Eyes doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no wallet connection, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share anything about you. The only things counted are anonymous totals, explained below. This page covers gatoreyes.fun, the Gator Eyes phone app, the Gator Eyes browser extension and the Gator Eyes database behind them. Gator Run Downs is a separate game with its own accounts, fair-play checks and privacy page.

Browser extension

What it reads
Only the address of the tab when you're on solada.fun or snek.fun, so it knows which coin page you're looking at. It never reads page contents, what you type on those pages, cookies, passwords or your wallet. The Gator Run Downs tab reads the races open for picks from gatorrundowns.fun and, if you're signed in to Gator Run Downs in the same browser, your username, weekly points and your picks on those races.
What it sends
On the market side, nothing about you, apart from questions you choose to ask Quagmire (below). It downloads the same public market data gatoreyes.fun uses (coin totals and recent alerts) and matches the coin on your own computer. The page address is never sent anywhere. On the Gator Run Downs tab, pressing Lunge or No Lunge sends that pick to gatorrundowns.fun. Your browser attaches your existing Gator Run Downs sign-in, the same as on the site; the extension never reads or stores it. From there the pick is handled under the Gator Run Downs privacy page and fair-play rules, exactly like a pick made on the site.
Ask Quagmire
The coin panel has an Ask Quagmire box. When you press a quick button or type a question and press Ask, that question, plus your previous question so follow-ups make sense, is sent to the Gator Eyes database to get Quagmire's answer. Nothing else goes with it: not the page address, your wallet or anything else on the page. Like the site helper, each question and answer is saved anonymously for up to 120 days to improve the answers, with no name, ID, wallet or IP address.
What it stores
Whether you collapsed the panel, whether the Ask Quagmire box is open, which popup tab you used last, whether race alerts are on, the ids of recent races so an alert isn't shown twice, and a copy of the public data for about a minute so it doesn't download it again. All of it stays in your browser.
Race alerts
Off unless you turn them on in the Gator Run Downs tab. Only then does the extension ask Chrome for permission to show notifications. To show the count on the toolbar icon, it checks Gator Run Downs' public list of open races about twice a minute.
Other sites
It doesn't run on any site except solada.fun and snek.fun, and it never changes or clicks anything on those pages.

gatoreyes.fun

Saved in your browser
Your watchlist, launchpad choice, beginner-mode setting and "since you last looked" snapshot are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you make a share link yourself or use watchlist phone alerts (see Phone alerts below).
No tracking
No analytics, no ad trackers and no third-party cookies. Gator Eyes doesn't follow you from page to page or site to site.
Anonymous counts
When you add Gator Eyes to your home screen or app list, and when you turn on phone alerts, the site adds one to a running total. All it sends is the type of device (iPhone, Android or computer) and the date. There's no name, ID, wallet, watchlist or IP address in the count, and it can't be linked back to you. A note in your browser stops the same device being counted twice.
Phone alerts
If you turn on phone alerts, the Gator Eyes server keeps your phone's push address (issued by Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, so it can deliver the notification) and the alert choices you picked. If you choose watchlist-only alerts or starred-coin trades, the coins on your watchlist are kept too, so the server knows which alerts to send. Nothing else about you is stored. Turning alerts off deletes it.
Service providers
The site is hosted on Render, the data feed runs on Cloudflare, fonts load from Fontshare and Google Fonts, and some Solana coin prices come from DexScreener. Like any web host, they may log technical details such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect their services.
Addresses you type
Addresses you enter in a tool, like the Holders page's leave-out list, are used in your browser for that view only. Gator Eyes doesn't save them.
Ask Quagmire
The site helper answers from the public data feed inside your browser. To improve its answers, each question and answer is saved anonymously for up to 120 days, with no name, ID, wallet or IP address. The chat on your screen clears when you close the tab.

Everything else

Children
Gator Eyes isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
Changes
If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
