Balances come from Cardano through Koios. Solada's own wallets are named from its proof-of-reserves page. Pool contracts are matched against published Splash and Minswap validators. The Snek.fun bonding curve is identified from its pool output. Spectrum/Splash lock dates are decoded from unspent on-chain outputs only when amounts reconcile with the holder balance; expired locks are not called active. The $burnsnek burn address stays in the observed-supply denominator. Unknown contracts remain unidentified. Solana-side balances are not included. Wallet links open Cardanoscan.
