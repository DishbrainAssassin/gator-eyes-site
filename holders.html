Holder balances come from the Cardano blockchain through Koios, with Solada's own labels for the curve, pool, locks and creator wallet. Solada's own wallets (reserve, orders, pool) are named from its proof-of-reserves page. Snek.fun coins get the creator wallet from the pool data and no other labels, so their curve and pool show up under "other contracts". Solana-side balances of dual-chain coins aren't included.
