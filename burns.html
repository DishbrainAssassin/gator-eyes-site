How this is checked

The Graveyard

The Cardano Graveyard is the $burnsnek address made by the SNEK team. It is a script that can never release anything, so tokens sent there are gone for good. Its balance of a coin is that coin's burned total. See the address on Cardanoscan.

Who sent it

Creator means the coin's own launcher wallet sent the burn. Solada means one of Solada's published wallets did (from its proof-of-reserves page). Anything else is shown as the sending address. A burn from a wallet is not an automatic burn unless Solada's wallet sent it.

Coverage

Every Solada coin and the $SOLADA platform token. The history starts Oct 8, 2026, the day before Solada's first coin; $SOLADA's total also includes burns from before then. New burns show up within about 5 minutes, and every total is re-read from the chain once a day. Burns to other addresses are not counted.

Value

≈ ADA is the burned share of supply times the coin's market cap right now, so it moves with price. It is what the burned tokens would be worth today, not what was paid for them. Burning does not change a coin's price by itself.

Burns, balances and senders come from Cardano through Koios. Coin names, market caps and burn settings come from Solada.fun. Transaction and address links open Cardanoscan; coin links open Solada with our referral code.
