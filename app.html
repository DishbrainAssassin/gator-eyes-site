Good to know

  • Alerts come from the same checks as Discord, every minute, with the same rules. If several land in the same minute, they arrive as one notification.
  • Tap a notification to open the live alerts list.
  • Need a break? Mute alerts on this page for 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 8 hours, 12 hours or 24 hours. They resume automatically, or you can unmute early. To stop alerts, turn them off on this page.
  • Nothing personal is kept. Your phone gives us an anonymous delivery address from Apple, Google or your browser, and we store it with the choices above. Turning alerts off deletes it. Gator Eyes alerts never ask you to connect a wallet, sign anything or send ADA. Coin names come from whoever launched the coin, so treat them as unverified; web links are stripped out of alerts and tapping one always opens gatoreyes.fun.
