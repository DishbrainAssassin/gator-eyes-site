Watching Solada

How Gator Eyes works

Gator Eyes is a free, automated tracker for Solada.fun. It reads every public trade, adds up where the money moves, and posts what stands out. No sign-up, no wallet connection.

Our promise

Free forever. Gator Eyes will never charge for access.

  • No paywall

    Every page, alert, tool and data feed stays open to everyone. Nothing gets moved behind a paid tier later.

  • No subscriptions

    No monthly plan, no premium alerts, no "pro" version. What you see is the whole thing.

  • No token gate

    You don't need to hold $GAEY or any other coin to use Gator Eyes.

  • No sign-up

    No account, no email, no wallet connection. Open the page and it works.

Three steps, every two minutes

  1. 1

    Read

    Pull the latest trades from Solada's public data (the Solada curve, Minswap, Splash and Pump.fun) and from Snek.fun's public data (its curve and graduated Splash pools).

  2. 2

    Tally

    Add each trade to the day's totals by coin, by hour and by venue. Days run midnight to midnight Eastern.

  3. 3

    Post

    Send anything that crosses a rule below to Discord and X, update the flows dashboard, and post Gator Tracks each morning.

Snek.fun coverage

Added October 2026

What's covered
Trades on the Snek.fun bonding curve and in the Splash pools coins graduate into. They show up in alerts, the flows dashboard, the tokens table and Gator Tracks, tagged Snek.fun.
Whale line
A Snek.fun trade is a whale trade at 1,000 ADA or more, or when it moves a large share of a small coin's market cap. The Solada rules are unchanged.
Pick a launchpad
The launchpad bar under the menu narrows every page to Solada or Snek.fun. "All" shows both together, with a "By launchpad" split in Gator Tracks and on the flows dashboard.
No referral
Snek.fun links carry no referral code. Gator Eyes earns nothing from Snek.fun trades.

What Gator Eyes doesn't do

  • No safety ratings

    Gator Eyes doesn't score coins, label rugs or call anything safe. It reports what traded.

  • No wallet tracking

    It doesn't follow, rank or name wallets. Wallet addresses in alerts link to the public transaction, nothing more.

  • No trading

    No swaps, no copy trading, no wallet connection. Every trade link opens Solada or Snek.fun itself.

  • No calls

    No "gem", "moon" or "buy now". Alerts describe what happened, not what to do next.
