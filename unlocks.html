How this is checked

Verified on-chain

Solada locks tokens at a Cardano script address that only opens for the owner's key after a set slot. Gator Eyes rebuilds that script from the owner's key and the date, hashes it, and compares with the address. A match proves who locked it, when it opens, and that the tokens are still there. Every row marked Verified on-chain passed that test.

Where the dates come from

For locks made after launch, the funding transaction carries a solada:lock message with the exact slot. Launch-time locks are matched against the creator's key and Solada's stated date. Dates are midnight UTC, which is the evening before in Eastern time, and the time shown on each row is Eastern.

Coverage

Solada's holders list shows a coin's top 100 holders. When Solada's own locked total is bigger than the locks in that list, Gator Eyes scans every holder address of the coin for lock scripts (at most once a day per coin). Every Solada coin with holders is checked about every two hours, biggest market cap first. Coins with zero holders and Snek.fun coins are not covered yet.

Value

≈ ADA is the lock's share of supply times the coin's market cap right now, so it moves with price. Locks are listed by the date they can be withdrawn. An open lock does not mean the owner will sell.
